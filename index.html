Research Profile

Ph.D. researcher in experimental physics specializing in acoustic tweezers, optical tweezers, micro-particle trapping, acoustofluidics, and advanced optical microscopy (bright-field, phase contrast, dark-field, and fluorescence). Currently focused on developing integrated optical-acoustic manipulation systems for cell sorting, non-invasive isolation of motile cells, and the optimization of acoustic resonators.
